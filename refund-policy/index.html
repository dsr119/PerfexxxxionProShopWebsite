Refund Policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

Most of what we sell is ordered for you and fitted to your hand, so our policy depends on whether the work has been done yet. If something isn't right, call us at (570) 815-1026 and we'll sort it out.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel a ball order at no charge until we've placed it with the distributor. After that, the rules for undrilled balls below apply.

Undrilled bowling balls

An undrilled ball can be returned within 14 days of pickup if it is unused and in its original packaging, with your receipt. We'll refund it the same way you paid. If the distributor charges us a return fee for a special-order ball, we may take that out of the refund, and we'll tell you the amount before you decide.

Drilled bowling balls

Once a ball is drilled to your measurements it can't be resold, so we can't refund it. What we do instead:

  • Fit adjustments: if the fit isn't comfortable, bring the ball back within 30 days and we'll adjust it (inserts, thumb, and span changes) at no charge.
  • Our mistake: if a ball wasn't drilled to the specs we agreed, we'll plug and redrill it, or replace it, at our cost.

Defective products

Cracks, cover defects, and other manufacturing problems are covered by the manufacturer's warranty. Bring the ball in and we'll file the claim for you. Replacements and refunds under a warranty are up to the manufacturer.

Accessories

Bags, shoes, and other accessories can be returned within 14 days if unused and in their original packaging, with your receipt. Grips, tape, inserts, and other items that have been fitted or used can't be returned.

Services and lessons

Drilling, resurfacing, and other services can't be refunded once done. If you can't make a lesson or appointment, please give us at least 24 hours' notice and we'll reschedule at no charge.

Discontinued balls

If we can't get a discontinued ball you ordered, the order is cancelled and you owe nothing.

How to ask for a refund

Call (570) 815-1026 or come into either shop with your receipt. Refunds go back to the original form of payment.

This policy doesn't take away any rights you have under Pennsylvania law.
