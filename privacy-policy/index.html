Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

Perfexxxion Pro Shop ("we", "us") is a bowling pro shop run by Keith and Amy Danielowski at Idle Hours South and Valley Lanes in Pennsylvania. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and in the shop, what we do with it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

When you order a ball on this website, we collect your name, your phone number, the ball and weight you chose, where you are ordering from and picking up, whether we already have your fitting specs on file, and when you sent the order.

When you order, book a drilling or fitting, or book a lesson by phone, in person, or through our Google Form, we collect the same kind of details, plus any notes needed for the job. For drilling we keep your fitting measurements and layout so future balls fit the same way.

When you send a data request, we collect what you put in it so we can act on it.

When you visit the site, your browser connects to the services that run it (listed below), and they receive your IP address and basic browser details, as any website does. With your permission only, we also use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. See our Cookie Policy.

We do not ask for or store payment card numbers, email addresses, home addresses, dates of birth, or government ID numbers through this website. We do not track your location, and we do not run advertising.

How we use it

  • To place, track, and fill your order, and to call you about it.
  • To schedule and carry out drilling, fitting, and lessons.
  • To keep your fitting specs and order history so we can help you with future equipment.
  • To run the website and keep it working (for example, showing this week's hours and the ball catalog).
  • With your permission, to understand how many people visit and which pages they use.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep order and fitting records for as long as you are a customer, so we can refer back to your equipment and specs. You can ask us to delete them at any time (see below). We may keep basic sales records for as long as tax and accounting rules require. Deleted information can stay in our encrypted backups until those backups are replaced.

Keeping it safe

We use established hosting providers and keep the people with access to customer records to those who need it to run the shop. No website or database is perfectly secure, so please don't send us anything sensitive (like card numbers) through the website.

Your choices

  • See, correct, or delete your information: use our data request page, call (570) 815-1026, or ask us in the shop. We will confirm it's you, usually by calling the phone number we have on file, and we aim to respond within 30 days.
  • Cookies and analytics: use the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of any page. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a "no" to analytics and the reviews widget.
  • Calls: we only call you about your own orders and appointments. Tell us if you'd rather we didn't.

Children

Keith coaches bowlers of all ages, but this website is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information online from them. A parent or guardian should place orders and book lessons for a child. If you think a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
