Cookie Policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

This site works without cookies or tracking. A few optional extras use them, and they only run if you say yes in the cookie banner. You can change your choice any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.

Your choices

  • Necessary only: nothing optional loads. Our Google reviews are still a click away through the "View Our Google Reviews" link.
  • Accept all: analytics and the reviews widget load.
  • Choose: pick each one separately.

If you turn analytics off after allowing it, we delete the Google Analytics cookies from your browser. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat that as "Necessary only". You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
