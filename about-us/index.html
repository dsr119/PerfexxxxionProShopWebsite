Columbia 300 staff

Owner · Ball driller

Keith Danielowski

The shop’s lead driller, focused on a comfortable, repeatable fit and equipment that matches how you bowl.

Read Keith’s story +

Keith first picked up a bowling ball at 11 in the Friday Junior Leagues at Idle Hours South—then known as South Side Bowl. By 15, coaches Tre Veltri Harris, Bill Ridgeway, the late Rich Heim, and later Joe Hutchinson helped him believe he could compete at the highest levels.

He rolled his first 300 on January 5, 1990 with a Columbia Black U Dot. A year later, he won the NY Junior Masters from the top seed after averaging 247 in qualifying. Through high school and college he added league titles, high-average honors, three more 300s, and two 800 series.

Keith entered the pro shop world with Dave Bainbridge at Bowler’s Alley, then ran the shop at Valley Lanes beginning in 1993. Today, returning to the pro shop feels like coming full circle—using a lifetime in bowling to build better fits, stronger games, and lasting connections.

Co-owner

Business manager

Amy Danielowski

The organizer behind both locations, keeping orders, appointments, stock, and the day-to-day shop experience rolling.

Read Amy’s story +

Amy started bowling in her early 20s in a Thursday league at South Side Bowl with her sister. She met Keith while selling 50/50 tickets to bowlers in the Times Major League, and her love for the game grew as she added leagues at Idle Hours, Valley Lanes, and Green Ridge Lanes.

Now co-owner and business manager of both shop locations, Amy keeps the shelves stocked, manages custom orders for balls, bags, shoes, and jerseys, schedules appointments and lessons, and handles the clerical details that keep Perfexxxxion moving.

Brunswick staff

Ball driller

James Durland

A lifelong bowler and experienced driller who helps customers find the right fit and equipment.

Read James’s story +

Bowling runs in James’s family. Across 29 years on the lanes, he has rolled 12 perfect games, three 800 series, and competed collegiately for Union College in Barbourville, Kentucky.

He began working in pro shops at 21 and has served bowlers at Pogo Family Pro Shop, Hudson Valley Bowling Supply, Strike It Rich Pro Shop, and now Perfexxxxion. Away from the lanes, James enjoys hunting, woodworking, building ball racks, and tinkering with anything mechanical.

Storm staff

Equipment specialist

Brian Wertz

The team’s SPI equipment resource, testing new Storm, Roto Grip, and 900 Global releases.

Read Brian’s story +

Brian started bowling at six in the Friday leagues at South Side Bowl, then stepped away to pursue soccer. After an injury ended that chapter, a coworker brought him back to the lanes at Idle Hours North.

Lessons and equipment work with Mike Markis grew into a bowling family that included Keith and Amy. Their mentorship helped Brian earn opportunities to represent Storm Products International and Turbo Grips, experience he now uses to guide customers toward the right equipment.

Shop technician

Doug Smith

Helps with ball plug, resurfacing, customer consultations, and whatever else keeps the shop moving.

Read Doug’s story +

Doug fell in love with bowling on a 2022 date night after seeing a nearby bowler hook the whole lane. Keith drilled his first ball—the IQ Tour Ruby—in 2023, and Doug taught himself to bowl two-handed that summer, finishing with a 206 average and 286 high game.

His breakout 2024–25 season included four sanctioned 300s, four more unsanctioned perfect games, and an 821 series. Grateful for the help Keith gave him, Doug joined the shop to offer customers that same kind of support.
